Mobile reader for books and documents that turns hard-to-read PDFs into reflowable text.

विकास में 2026

यह पेज अभी आपकी भाषा में अनूदित नहीं है, इसलिए अंग्रेज़ी में दिखाया गया है।

A reading app for iPhone and Android with a server-side converter: PDFs and other awkward formats become reflowable FB2, so they read comfortably on a phone.

  • Mobile app: Vue 3 + Vuetify + Capacitor, rendering with foliate-js.
  • Portal and API on Ruby on Rails 8: accounts, sync, plans and subscriptions, owner-only admin.
  • Separate Python conversion service with background jobs.
